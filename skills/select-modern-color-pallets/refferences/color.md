## 1. Architectural Rules for Modern Color Systems

Modern color schemes on Coolors strictly depart from generic saturated primaries and generic AI zinc templates. They follow deliberate mathematical and perceptual harmonies:

1. **The 60-30-10 Distribution**:
   - **60% (Base/Canvas)**: Grounding tone (charcoal slate, deep sea navy, or warm alabaster/linen).
   - **30% (Structure/Cards)**: Elevated surface layers, hairline borders, and subtle card fills.
   - **10% (Focal Action/CTA)**: High-chroma punch (burnt sienna, vibrant amber, vermilion, emerald teal).
2. **Desaturated Foundations**: Neutral shades avoid pure grayscale; they carry subtle warm undertones (bone, linen, oat) or cool undertones (marine slate, sage fog).
3. **Simple 2-Stop Gradients Only**: Gradients strictly use subtle tonal transitions (e.g. matching hue shifted by 5-10% lightness) or low-opacity ambient glows (5%–12%). **No multi-color rainbow ramps.**

## 5. Summary Checklist
- [ ] Never fall back to generic `#09090B` / `#6366F1` / `#06B6D4` templates.
- [ ] Gradients must stay strictly **2-stop and tonal** (no noisy rainbow ramps).
- [ ] Ensure all text passes WCAG 2.1 AA (contrast >= 4.5:1 for standard body text).
